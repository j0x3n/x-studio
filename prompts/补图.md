# AI 补图和高清化的提示词

给后端的 enhance 步骤用。按编号取对应段落，把 `{info}` 换成这款的款名、品类、尺寸、颜色、亮点（一行中文），参考图用这款已有的实拍照片。所有生成都走图像编辑接口（带参考图），不用纯文生图，这样商品外观以实拍为准。

## 通用要求（拼在每段提示词最前面）

This is a product photo for a freshwater pearl jewelry shop. The reference image shows the exact product. Reproduce the product exactly as in the reference: the same earring design, the same metal parts and stones, the same pearl size, shape, color and luster. Do not redesign, do not add or remove any parts, do not change proportions. No text, no logo, no watermark, no frame. Make it look like an honest phone photo taken by a small shop owner, not a studio render or CGI: natural soft window light, true-to-life colors, slight natural shadows, a little real-world texture. Keep the pearl's natural character: subtle surface marks, slight unevenness in shape and luster as in the reference; do not make the pearl perfectly round, glossy or flawless; do not make the metal or stones look brand-new and flawless. No beautifying.

## 高清化（upscale）

Enhance this exact photo. Keep everything identical: composition, framing, product, person, background, lighting and colors. Only reduce blur, noise and compression artifacts and recover fine detail. Keep all natural imperfections of the pearl, metal and skin; do not smooth or beautify. Do not add, remove or change anything.

## 规则（后端照做，不给模型看）

- **只给这些编号补**：A1、E1、E2、E3、D4。
- **不补的编号**，缺了只提醒补拍：
  - E5（托盘批量实拍）、B1（生长纹微距）：这两张是"真珍珠"的证据，生成的不算数，还会和"原相机实拍"的说法冲突。
  - C 组（尺子、硬币、多尺寸上耳）：生成的尺寸参照会误导买家。
  - F 组（包装）：不能生成实际不存在的包装。
- **参考图怎么选**：优先 A1、A2、F2、E4、B2、B3（能看清整只商品的）；上耳图 D4 再加一张已有的 D1–D3。
