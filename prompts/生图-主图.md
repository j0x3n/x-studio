# AI 主图的提示词

给 creative 模块的 `creative.main` 用。每张图的最终提示词 = **通用要求** + 对应**商品模式**那一段 + 模板 `prompt`（占位符已替换）+ 重画时的**上一次的问题**。模板文件在 `templates/生图模板/main/`。

## 通用要求

This is a main listing image for a small online jewelry shop selling freshwater pearl and crystal jewelry ({{category_name}}). It must look like a real photograph taken by a careful product photographer, not a 3D render, not CGI, not an illustration. Surface, background, wardrobe, light and color mood follow the scene described below; if no scene is given, use the shop default: cool grey product layer, cool white side light, low-key and calm. Always: generous asymmetric negative space, fine natural texture, a little film grain. Metal parts are always silver-toned exactly as in the reference; never add a warm or golden tint to the metal. Forbidden: pink or yellow color cast, bright gold, gradients, sparkles, lens flares, bokeh hearts, flowers, petals, ribbons, confetti, glossy plastic look. Keep the natural character of pearls and stones: growth lines, small surface marks, slight unevenness of shape and luster, inclusions and color zoning; do not make them perfectly round, perfectly clear or flawless. No beautifying. No text, no logo, no watermark, no border, no collage unless this prompt explicitly asks for text.

## pixel_lock 像素锁定

The input image already contains the exact product placed on a neutral grey canvas. The product area is locked and will be pasted back pixel for pixel afterwards, so do not move, resize, recolor, relight or redraw the product, and do not paint anything over it. Only create the surroundings: surface, background, light falloff and a soft natural contact shadow that matches the product's position and the side light. The light direction in the new scene must match the highlights already visible on the product. Do not add any other jewelry, props or hands that overlap the product.

## model 模特

Use the person in the model reference images exactly: the same identity, skin tone, skin texture, hair and nails. Use the product reference images exactly: the same number of pieces, segments and beads, the same bead size and shape, color, luster, metal color, clasp and every connector. Do not add or remove any part, do not redesign. Do not show the full face; crop as the framing below says, but keep the chin (and for necklaces the lower lip) in frame so the neck has a scale reference. Keep a natural, average adult neck length and shoulder line as in the model reference; head upright, chin relaxed, never tilted back; do not elongate or slim the neck. Skin keeps real texture and pores, no airbrushing. Wardrobe and background follow the styling given below; if none is given: charcoal or black knit sweater or crew neck, dark blue-grey backdrop, side light, low-key. No other jewelry on the model. Wear position: {佩戴位置}

## redraw 参考图重画

Reproduce the product from the reference images exactly: the same design, parts, sizes, colors and luster. Do not redesign, do not add or remove parts, do not change proportions. Only the scene, angle and lighting are new. The reference photos are real product photos and only show what the product looks like: ignore their background, gift box, cushion, hands, grey padding and lighting, unless the scene below asks for the packaging.

## none 不放商品

There is no product in this image. Do not draw any jewelry.

## 比例

{比例} Bead size and bead count matter more than anything else in this image: buyers compare them with the real piece. Judge the size against the anchors above, not against the reference photo's framing, and match the density of beads in the reference.

## 上一次的问题

The previous attempt was rejected for these reasons. Fix them this time and keep everything else as specified: {问题}

## 规则（后端照做，不给模型看）

- `{{category_name}}` 由 `core.FillPrompt` 替换；`{佩戴位置}` 按方案 3.3 节的表取（`creative` 代码里的映射），`{问题}` 是上一次复核的 issues 原因加人工 note，用「；」连起来。
- 模板 `prompt` 里的 `{文字}` 有值时，**删掉通用要求最后一句**（"No text … unless this prompt explicitly asks for text." 保留后半句即可），并在提示词末尾加：`Render the following Chinese text exactly, character by character, no extra characters, no typos, crisp and legible: ` + 逐条带引号的文字。
- 「比例」一节只在 `model`、`redraw` 模式、且算得出珠径时加，`{比例}` 由 `creative/scale.go` 的 `beadScale` 按珠径（资料 `size`，取范围中值）、链长（上架参数「链长/长度/手围」行或亮点里的 cm）、款式算出：满串颗数 = 链长 ÷ 珠径，佩戴正面按半串换算（项链和手链都只看得到约一半），耳垂按 17 mm 换算。
- `{商品}` 按品类字段的中文名和单位写（如「珠径：3.3–3.8 mm」），不带 `auto_*` 开关。
- `photo` 模式不调生图，不用本文件。
- 首图 B 版：同一模板再生成一次，末尾加 `This is an alternative version B: use a clearly different camera angle and composition from version A while keeping the same style.`
