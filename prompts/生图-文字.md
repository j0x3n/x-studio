# 改字的提示词

给 creative 的 `POST /api/creative/items/{id}/retext` 用：mask 只开放要改的那块文字区域（OCR 的 box 外扩 10%），其余地方保留，生成后照常跑文字核对。

## 提示词

Edit only the transparent (masked) area of this image. In that area there is a piece of text that is wrong. Replace it with exactly this Chinese text: “{{text}}”. Render it character by character, no extra characters, no missing characters, no typos, no pseudo characters. Keep the same typeface style, weight, size, color, letterspacing, orientation (horizontal or vertical) and alignment as the original text in that area, so the result looks like it was always there. Keep the background texture behind the text continuous with its surroundings. Do not change anything outside the editable area. The previous text in this area was: “{{old_text}}”. {{hint}}

## 规则（后端照做，不给模型看）

- `{{text}}`：新的字（请求里的 `text`）；`{{old_text}}`：OCR 在这个 box 里读到的原文，没有就空串；`{{hint}}`：数字或单位时填 `Numbers and units must be exactly as given.`，否则空串。
- 改字用 `EditImageMask`，尺寸用这张图的生成尺寸；改完后 OCR 区域以外的像素按 mask 贴回原图（和像素锁定一样，避免整张图被悄悄改动）。
- 改字算一次生成，占 `images_used`。
