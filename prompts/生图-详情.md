# AI 详情页的提示词

给 creative 模块的 `creative.detail` 用。每屏最终提示词 = **通用要求** + `生图-主图.md` 里对应**商品模式**那一段 + 模板 `prompt`（占位符已替换）+ **图上的字** + 重画时 `生图-主图.md` 的**上一次的问题**。模板文件在 `templates/生图模板/detail/`。

## 通用要求

This is one screen of a vertical product detail page for a small online jewelry shop selling freshwater pearl and crystal jewelry ({{category_name}}). It must look like a poster laid out by a designer in Photoshop, not a web page: one strong full-bleed photograph, text set directly on the image, asymmetric generous negative space, subtle film grain. No UI elements, no buttons, no icons, no rounded cards, no drop shadows on text boxes, no bullet lists, no screenshots. Typography: product name in a large elegant Song / Ming serif typeface (vertical setting allowed), small supporting text in a light sans-serif, letterspacing a little open, text color off-white or charcoal depending on the background. Gold is only allowed for a small "MYSTIC LUSTRE" wordmark or a thin corner mark, never for photos or large areas. Shop palette: dark brand layer (deep navy #1A2340, charcoal) and cool grey product layer, cool white side light, low-key. Forbidden: pink, beige or warm cast, bright gold, gradients, sparkles, lens flares, flowers, petals, ribbons. Product photos must look real and keep natural imperfections of pearls and stones; metal is always silver-toned.

## 图上的字

Render only the following Chinese text, exactly character by character, no extra words, no typos, no pseudo characters, crisp and legible at 750 px width. Place it where the layout description says. Text: {文字}

If there is no text to render, render no text at all.

## 规则（后端照做，不给模型看）

- 每屏生成 1024×1536（模板 `gen_size`），导出缩到 750 宽。
- `{文字}` 是这屏模板 `texts` 解析出来的字，逐条带中文引号、用「 / 」分隔，并在每条前标注位置（模板 `texts[].key`），例如 `【款名】“初雪” / 【一句话】“像清晨落在窗台的第一层霜”`。
- 参数类（含数字、mm、cm、925、元、¥）的字按 critical 处理，比对不过不能导出。生成时提示词末尾再加一句：`Numbers and units must be exactly as given.`
